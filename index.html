How to play

  • Your pucks are orange (bottom). The rival's are blue (top).
  • Press on one of your pucks, pull back like a slingshot, and release to shoot.
  • Pucks only change sides through the gate in the middle wall.
  • Empty your side first to win. Faster wins and fewer shots = bigger score.
